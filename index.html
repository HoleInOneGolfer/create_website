About me

I am Benjamin Bowles, a Computer Science undergraduate pursuing a B.S. with a Minor in Mathematics at Texas Tech University (Class of 2027). I have a strong foundation in software engineering, full-stack application development, and machine learning.

During my academic career, I have maintained a 3.368 GPA, earned placement on the Fall 2023 Dean’s Honor List, and received the Presidential Merit Scholarship. My technical skillset includes Python, Java, C, JavaScript, NodeJS, HTML5/CSS, Docker, Linux, and Git/GitHub.

My hands-on project experience includes developing full-stack web applications and machine learning models. I worked as a Software Developer Intern at the San Antonio Museum of Science and Technology (SAMSAT), where I built an Interactive Museum Quiz Kiosk Server using JavaScript and Node.js. Additionally, I have designed an analytical Power Output Estimator ML model and built custom PyTorch neural network weather predictors.

Beyond software development, I hold a strong background in leadership, logistical organization, and project management. My experiences include directing my Eagle Scout project for the Texas Transportation Museum and working as camp staff for the Alamo Area Council Cub Scout Summer Day Camp.
